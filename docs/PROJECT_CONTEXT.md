## Who This Is For (product context)

CrossCheck is being built for marketing/compliance teams (e.g. in
education/university marketing, illustrated by the "Online Manipal → MUJ →
MBA" example in `docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md`) who need to keep a large number of
marketing assets (landing pages, brochures, emails, WhatsApp templates)
factually consistent with authoritative institutional sources, at a scale
where manual checklists don't hold up.
